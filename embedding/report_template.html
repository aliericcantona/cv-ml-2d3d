Metric definitions

Query and ranking
Every part is used once as a query. All other parts are ranked by cosine similarity of their part embeddings, most similar first; the query itself is excluded. A result is relevant when it has the same class (MCB folder) as the query. All metrics are averaged over queries.
P@k (precision at k)
Fraction of the top-k results that are relevant. P@1 asks "is the single closest part from the same class?"; P@10 asks how clean the 10 closest are.
R@k (Recall@K)
Fraction of queries with at least one relevant result in the top k (the "Recall@K" of metric-learning papers). Useful for lookup, where the right answer only has to appear near the top.
AP / mAP
Average precision: the precision at the rank of each relevant result, averaged over all relevant results of the query, so it scores the whole ranking. mAP is the mean AP over queries; the strictest number here.
k-NN accuracy
Leave-one-out classification: predict the majority class of the k nearest parts (ties go to the nearest) and compare with the true class.
Chance P@k
Expected P@k of a random ranking: 1 / number of classes (with equal class sizes).
Most confused with
The class a class's parts are most often predicted as by the k-NN classifier.
Per-class scores
The same metrics averaged only over queries from that class.
